## 🚀 Key Gameplay Features

* ⛏️ **Vanilla-Plus Survival:** Raw survival mechanics with optimized multiplayer stability.
* ⚔️ **PvP & Rivalries:** Form tight alliances, defend your territory, and handle rival battles fairly.
* 💥 **Creeper Control System:** Built-in server safeguards to ensure your massive builds and hard work don't get griefed by accident.
* 💰 **Player Economy:** Set up community trade networks, open player-run shops, and accumulate server wealth.
* 🏠 **Custom Towns & Bases:** Ample world boundaries for players to build massive kingdoms or secret underground bunkers.

## 📜 Server Guidelines & Rules

To keep the gameplay environment fun and fair for everyone on the Backbench, please follow these rules:
1. **No Hacking or Cheating:** Modified clients, X-ray packs, or movement exploits are strictly prohibited.
2. **No Toxic Griefing:** Respect player boundaries. Stealing or destroying claims without an active in-game war/lore reason will lead to an immediate ban.
3. **Play Fair:** Keep the chat friendly and welcoming to all new members joining the community.
